BOTTOM SCORES
- Loads the full Monday–Sunday calendar week in Eastern Time. The chosen date
  selects its containing week; a blank date follows the current week.
- Includes all scheduled games returned by the selected league feeds, including
  MLB doubleheaders. No OFF cards for teams without scheduled games.
- UFL, NFL, MLB, NBA, MLS, Division I football and basketball remain available.
- Full matchup cards slide to a single-team stat card. That card preserves the
  original team's logo, name, record and score; winners retain yellow text.
  The other team block is replaced by the player's stats. A home player's card
  correctly uses the home team rather than the unrelated away team.
- Provider leader rows now retain their reported team association, including
  QB leaders such as Dak. Unknown team associations are never guessed.
- Completed games skip their TV/network detail row. League title-deck branding
  remains controlled by the league's title settings.
- Same-card refreshes preserve in-progress slides and league-deck collapse.
  Interrupted outgoing cards retain their visible position, and superseded
  score digits/scrolls are cleaned up. Motion remains sliding, not a 3D flip.
- The independent feed scorebug moves above the main scorebug when both are on,
  and honors an explicit zero bottom offset.
