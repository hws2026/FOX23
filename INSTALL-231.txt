BOTTOM SCORE UPDATES
- Choose the leagues you want before showing the feed. UFL is now included.
- Every scheduled matchup returned by the selected feeds is retained, including
  MLB doubleheaders. Teams without a scheduled game do not receive OFF cards.
- Official and ESPN schedules are combined; a partial official schedule no
  longer discards games supplied only by ESPN. Distinct events stay distinct.
- College coverage is Division I: FBS + FCS football and Division I basketball.
- Loaded game counts are shown per league so you can check schedule coverage.
- All selected league cards appear, then all games and their available details
  in that league. The full deck returns; the completed card slides out and the
  next league comes in. Rotation repeats after the last league.
- The independent Feed scorebug can follow that rotation or a pinned game. It
  retains the previous game during transition decks, including the final deck.
- Matchup changes get a quick cool-white/blue light sweep. Team/logo/color and
  score updates light the affected section. Clock-only updates do not flash.
- The bottom game/stat cards keep their sliding motion. Player rows retain team
  abbreviations. Down/distance excludes the team/yard already shown in BALL ON.
- Baseball innings, soccer time and college basketball periods use the feed's
  status wording. Delays/halftime do not retain live field indicators. Overtime
  finals retain their overtime label. Missing data is not invented.

AUDIT FIXES
- Local edits report success only after browser storage commits. Failed saves
  roll back instead of silently leaving edits for a later poll to save.
- Untouched exported shows can be imported with catalog and play-clock metadata.
- Imports preserve assets, logos, lineups, catalog statistics, transition presets,
  countdown settings, network motion, saved graphics and valid saved-team links.
- Older playlist cues no longer overwrite newer saved-graphic settings.
- Import does not create false scoring flashes or a stale play-clock hide timer.
- Empty-show backups keep their empty settings. Outputs restore cleared and
  clocks paused. Unknown custom-library IDs remain detached with teams intact.
